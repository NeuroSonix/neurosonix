Applications

Selective Listening Across Devices and Platforms

NeuroSonix develops brain-inspired audio AI that enables machines to isolate and track a target voice in complex, noisy environments. Our technology is designed to bring selective listening to hearing devices, consumer electronics, voice systems and embedded audio platforms.

Hearing & Assistive Technology

Following a conversation becomes more difficult when several people speak at once. Hearing technology is an initial focus for NeuroSonix, with an approach designed to isolate a target speaker while preserving spatial audio cues.

Potential applications include hearing aids and assistive listening devices that help users focus on a selected voice while retaining the surrounding auditory context.

Our core technology operates with two microphones, offering a path toward selective listening within compact device designs.

Automatic Speech Recognition & Voice AI

Speech recognition systems can struggle when voices overlap or background sounds compete with the intended speaker. NeuroSonix provides a selective listening layer before automatic speech recognition, separating the target voice from the surrounding audio scene.

Potential applications include transcription systems, voice assistants and conversational AI that need to process speech in environments with multiple speakers.

Controlled evaluations described in our research materials demonstrate reduced speech recognition errors when our core audio processing technology is applied before recognition.

Semiconductor & Edge Audio Platforms

NeuroSonix is designed for integration into digital signal processing platforms and embedded audio systems. Its architecture targets real-time, low-power processing on the device, without cloud dependency.

For semiconductor and hardware partners, this creates an opportunity to incorporate sound separation and target selection into platforms used across multiple device categories.

Our planned integration models include embedded software licensing, semiconductor IP integration and SDK licensing for manufacturer software stacks.

Hearables & Consumer Audio

Earbuds, headsets and other consumer audio devices operate in changing acoustic environments, where several voices and sounds may compete for attention.

Potential applications include listening modes that focus on a selected speaker and voice interfaces that receive a more isolated speech signal. NeuroSonix’s two-microphone approach is relevant to devices where size, power consumption and hardware complexity matter.

Smart Speakers, Robotics & Security Systems

Selective listening also has potential applications in smart speakers, robotics and security systems that encounter sound from multiple sources.

By isolating and tracking a target voice or sound, NeuroSonix could provide a focused audio signal for downstream recognition, interpretation or interaction.

Built for Integration

NeuroSonix’s initial commercialization focus is hearing technology, automatic speech recognition and semiconductor platforms integrating edge audio AI.

The applications described here represent intended integration opportunities. Product availability and performance will depend on the hardware platform, application requirements and further validation.
